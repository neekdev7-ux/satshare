How It Works

Three steps. Full control, start to finish.

You decide when sharing is on, how much data it may use, and when it stops.

1

Install the Android app

Download the official SatShare Android app and sign in with your account. Bandwidth sharing is only possible through this authorized app — never from a browser.

2

Explicitly opt in to share

Review the consent screen, set your Wi-Fi-only preference and daily/monthly data limits, then tap Start. You can pause or stop sharing at any time.

3

Earn SAT, withdraw via Lightning

Verified bandwidth earns a share of actual collected customer revenue. Withdraw your SAT to any BOLT11 invoice or Lightning Address.

Sharing stays disabled until an approved customer destination is configured. If there is no authorized buyer, there is no sharing — and no earnings.

Features

Built for real rewards — and real protection.

Every design choice starts from consent, verification, and financial honesty.

Authorized traffic only

Only approved commercial destinations on a strict allowlist. No arbitrary third-party traffic, no unrestricted public proxy.

Lightning-fast payouts

Withdraw SAT over the Bitcoin Lightning Network via BOLT11 invoices or Lightning Addresses (LNURL-pay).

Revenue-based rewards

A configurable share of actual collected customer revenue funds the reward pool. No revenue, no rewards — and we say so plainly.

Privacy by design

Your private IP and sensitive device details are never exposed to customers. Traffic is encrypted and accounted server-side.

You set the limits

Daily and monthly data caps, Wi-Fi-only mode, and battery monitoring. Sharing respects the boundaries you choose.

Verified, auditable ledger

Every SAT movement is recorded in an immutable ledger. Server-side verification only — client-reported numbers are never trusted alone.

Referral rewards

Earn a configurable commission on verified rewards from people you refer — paid only after their contribution is verified.

Decentralized network

Contributors around the world power an authorized bandwidth network for verified commercial customers.

Fraud & abuse prevention

Signed sessions, duplicate and replay detection, and abuse controls keep rewards going to genuine contributors.
